For parents

Pick
Draw on white paper and take the photo in a bright place, without shadows. Everything the same color as the paper is erased automatically. The photo is processed only on this device and is never uploaded.
How to play
Trace a line on the screen with your finger and the character runs along it. Poke the character and it goes "boing" – poke it 7 times and… "POP!". You can also grab it and drag it around.
Can’t pick a photo
On some devices, photo picking doesn’t work when this page is opened inside another app. If so, open the link in your phone’s browser (Chrome or Safari).
Friends & food
In "Pick", add more drawings and they join as friends (up to 4). Drag a food drawing to a character’s mouth and it munches it up. Tap a friend to put a yellow ▼ on it, then control it with the buttons below (heart = go say hi). Tap the main character to switch back. Friends sometimes greet each other or go eat food lying around on their own.
Police
Press "Police" in "Pick" to bring out a police car. Use the buttons below for lights, horn and siren, and drive it around the screen. Press "Sample" to go back.
